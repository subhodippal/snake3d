How to play

⌨️ Desktop

Arrow keys / WASD to turn.

Hold the direction key you're moving in to sprint (or hold Space / Shift).

P / Esc to pause.

📱 Mobile

Swipe up / down / left / right to turn.

Tap & hold anywhere to sprint.

Prefer buttons? Turn on Button mode in Settings for a 4-way D-pad and a boost button.

🎯 Goal

Eat fruit to grow. Each level has its own goals: a number of 🍎 normal food, ★ special food (pizza, cake, burger, donut), and/or 🐭 small animals to catch (mouse, frog, bug, crab: they all run at the start of the level and never come back).

Food only lasts a little while (watch its ring shrink), then it vanishes and something new appears somewhere else.

Finish every goal and a hidden 🚪 door opens in the fence. Slither through it to reach the next level!

Every bump costs ❤️ health: the 🎋 fence, trees, rocks and the river all stop you and hurt a little. You can't bite yourself: your own tail is a wall too. If you get trapped, you are put back where you last ate. 👾 Slimes, 🐊 crocodiles, 🚗 traffic and the 🐺🐗🦂🕷️🦀 animals that guard their dens bite hard. At zero health it's game over.

☠️ Poison pools, 🔥 camp fires and 🌋 lava drain your health for every second you stay in them: slither around them! Each level hides only one or two 💖 Live Spells, so save them for when you need them. A 🕶️ Blind Spell hides everything except the ground for a few seconds.

💦 Snakes can't swim here! Cross rivers on the 🌉 bridges. Once the gate opens, a green arrow shows you the way.

Sprinting while you eat gives double points!
